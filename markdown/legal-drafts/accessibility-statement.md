# Accessibility & Non-Discrimination Statement

**Last Updated:** April 20, 2026

Tennessee Center for Laser Dentistry (TNCLD) is committed to ensuring our website and our practice are accessible to people with disabilities. We do not discriminate on the basis of disability and will make reasonable accommodations to provide effective communication and equal access to our services.

---

## Website Accessibility

### Our commitment

We strive to conform with **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** — the standard recommended by the U.S. Department of Justice under Title III of the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA).

Our efforts include:

- Regular accessibility audits using automated and manual testing
- Ongoing remediation of identified issues
- Training our web team on accessibility best practices
- Designing and developing new pages with accessibility in mind from the start

### Current status

We are actively working to bring all areas of our website into full conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Some content may not yet meet the standard in every area; remediation is ongoing.

Most recent audit: **April 20, 2026**

### Third-party content

Some content on our website is provided by third parties (for example, embedded maps, videos, or payment portals). We do not control the accessibility of third-party content but make reasonable efforts to select partners who prioritize accessibility.

## Effective Communication (ADA Title III, 28 CFR § 36.303)

### Auxiliary aids and services available at no charge

If you need assistance communicating with our staff, we will provide appropriate auxiliary aids and services **free of charge**, including:

- **Sign language interpreters** (American Sign Language — with reasonable advance notice)
- **Written materials in alternative formats** — large print, audio recording, or accessible electronic formats
- **Assistive listening devices** available during appointments
- **Communication via email, text, or written notes** if preferred

### To request an accommodation

Please let us know when scheduling your appointment, or as early as possible:

- **Phone:** (615) 595-8070
- **TTY users:** dial **711** (Tennessee Relay Service)
- **Email:** accessibility@tncld.com

We ask for reasonable advance notice (typically 5 business days for sign language interpreters) so we can arrange the service. For urgent needs, call us and we will do our best to accommodate same-day or next-day.

### Alternative format on request

Any information on this website, or any form normally completed on paper, is available in an alternative format on request at no charge. Call **(615) 595-8070** or email **accessibility@tncld.com**.

## Non-Discrimination

Tennessee Center for Laser Dentistry provides dental care and website information without discrimination on the basis of disability, race, color, national origin, age, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, religion, or veteran status.

All patients and prospective patients are entitled to equal access to our services.
